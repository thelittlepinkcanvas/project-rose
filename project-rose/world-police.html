Foraville Police

The flower on the emblem is a Rudbeckia flower, which is a symbol of justice. The emblem with no darkened Rudbeckia petals represents the national police as a whole. This symbol appears on police hats.

The Foraville national police has 10 departments, represented by each petal of the flower:
1. Criminal investigation department
2. Public safety
3. Intelligence division
4. Cybercrime department
5. Public affairs
6. SWAT
7. Forensics department
8. Internal affairs
9. Traffic department
10. Administration

Every department has their own emblem with one petal darkened to represent them. The specified logos appear on police IDs.
