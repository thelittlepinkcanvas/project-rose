Symbolism

The Foraville national flag was designed by Aster Sunfloria.

The five inner petals of the flower symbolises the 5 regions of Foraville:

Yellow - Sunflower District
Violet - Lavender District
Blue - Bluebell District
Light pink - Periwinkle's Farm
Pink - Lotus Dock

The red in the background symbolises the power users of the world, while the blue symbolises non-power users.

The color purple of the outer petals symbolises power users and non-power users united in Foraville, which is quite ironic in the current generation.

The flower is enclosed with black lines, representing the formation of the country among power and non-power users.
